# Lapis -- How the game plays

The story bible keeps the twist (you are the memory of the drowned Keeper, inside Mara's mind). The player still needs a clear surface goal from minute one. This is that goal.

## What the player is told

> Your daughter is lost at sea, drifting under the brightest star.
> Her sky is going dark: one by one, her stars are falling into the sea.
> Put them back. Light her sky again, and she can find her way home.

Told by the storybook, the little light's first lines, and the pause-menu journal. The twist (it's her mind; you are a memory; "home" means letting her go) stays for the ending.

## The goal

The sky has **five constellations**, one per region. Each is dark until you light it.

| Region | Constellation | Its stars come from |
|---|---|---|
| Coral Shore (spawn) | The Boat (6) | fallen stars on the shore and out at sea |
| Moonlit Meadows | The Hand (8) | 5 fallen, 1 in the Moon Tree, 2 behind puzzles |
| Lantern Wood | The Lantern (8) | 5 fallen, 1 in the Mushroom Ring, 2 behind puzzles |
| Glass Dunes | The Whale (8) | 5 fallen, 1 in the whale skeleton, 2 behind puzzles |
| Drowned Town | The Bell (8) | 5 fallen, 1 by the drowned bell, 2 behind puzzles |

38 stars in all.

A star only counts once it is **carried to a lighthouse** and lifted back into the sky from the lamp room at the top.

**Win:** all five constellations lit. The brightest star blazes, the River of Light opens, and you row north to the end of the world for the ending.

## The loop

1. Wake. The little light points you at the nearest unlit constellation's region.
2. Find stars: lying in the open, inside wonders, behind puzzles.
3. Carry them. While carried they glow, and **the night presses in**.
4. Reach a lighthouse, climb it, lift the stars from the lamp room, and rest: the night turns back, by less each
   time you rest at the same tower.
5. If the night runs out, she freezes, or a nightmare reaches you, carried stars fall back where you found them.

Along the way, keep her warm (only real light warms; the cold shows as frost, breath and shaking hands) and keep
your light (gathered from the glow, spent on flares). Dark Water and squalls close in as the night wears on. The
full survival design, and the Wander / Story / Survival modes, are in [SURVIVAL.md](SURVIVAL.md).

## Danger: nightmares

Her despair. As each night grows late, ink creatures come out of the dark away from light:
- **Wraiths** drift; **Crawlers** stalk, lunge and rest.
- They prowl until they hear or see you: running is loud, creeping (C) in dark grass is nearly silent and unseen.
  Hunting eyes blaze; lose them and they search where you were, then give up.
- Late in the night they come in packs, and flankers try to get between you and the nearest light.
- Near them the dream curdles: colour drains, the paint goes black and red, the music sours.
- A touch scatters your carried stars, once per encounter.
- Light drives them off: lit lighthouses, stone lanterns, and the little light's flare (Q, costs carried light).
- Where they linger, black thorns grow; lighting that region's constellation burns them away.

## Puzzles

Small, readable, and in-world, each guarding a star:
- **Lantern circles:** a ring of stone lanterns that must be lit in the order of the tune the little light hums.
- **Constellation stones:** standing stones carved with stars; turn them to match a constellation in the sky.
- **Echo poses:** a memory echo shows where Mara once stood; stand there at the right moment.
- **Bells:** in the Drowned Town, ring the sunken bells in the order the tide shows.
